1. unconscious /ʌnˈkɒnʃəs/

Definition: Not within thought; not awake.

Sentence: Lozanov’s instructional technique is based on the evidence that the connections made in the brain through unconscious processing are more durable than conscious ones.

2. suggestopedia /səˌdʒɛstəˈpiːdiə/

Definition: A teaching method that uses suggestion, music, and peripheral focus for learning.

Sentence: In suggestopedia, the curriculum becomes peripheral and is processed by the brain's reserve capacity.

3. peripheral /pəˈrɪfərəl/

Definition: Relating to or situated on the edge or periphery of something.

Sentence: We often remember what we have perceived peripherally, long after forgetting what we set out to learn.

4. hypnosis /hɪpˈnəʊsɪs/

Definition: A trance-like state of heightened focus and concentration.

Sentence: Lozanov experimented with teaching by direct suggestion during hypnosis, but found it unnecessary.

5. placebo /pləˈsiːbəʊ/

Definition: A substance or method with no therapeutic effect, often used as a control in testing new drugs.

Sentence: Lozanov acknowledged that the ritual surrounding suggestion in his system is also a placebo.

6. paraconsciously /ˌpærəˈkɒnʃəsli/

Definition: Learning or processing information beyond conscious awareness.

Sentence: The teacher’s task is to assist students to apply what they have learned paraconsciously.

7. trance /trɑːns/

Definition: A half-conscious state characterized by an absence of response to external stimuli.

Sentence: Lozanov experimented with suggestion during sleep, trance states, and hypnosis.

8. associative /əˈsəʊsɪətɪv/

Definition: Relating to or involving the association of ideas or concepts.

Sentence: Suggestopedia relies on associative memory by connecting language to emotions and music.

9. dramatization /ˌdræmətaɪˈzeɪʃən/

Definition: The process of acting out or presenting ideas in a theatrical way.

Sentence: Follow-up classes included dramatizations to help students recall vocabulary.

10. cognition /kɒɡˈnɪʃən/

Definition: The mental processes involved in gaining knowledge and understanding.

Sentence: The method utilizes both cognition and unconscious processing for effective learning.

11. mantra /ˈmæn.trə/

Definition: A word, phrase, or sound repeated to aid concentration in meditation.

Sentence: Teachers use soothing mantras to create a calming learning environment.

12. solemnly /ˈsɒləmli/

Definition: In a serious, formal, and dignified manner.

Sentence: The teacher reads the text solemnly, in sync with classical music.

13. faith /feɪθ/

Definition: Complete trust or confidence in something or someone.

Sentence: Some students lack the faith in suggestopedia as a teaching method.
