Let's Go Bats - Vocabulary

1. nocturnal /nɒkˈtɜːnəl/

Definition: Active at night.

Sentence: Bats are nocturnal creatures that hunt in the dark to avoid competition with daytime predators.

2. maneuver /məˈnuːvər/

Definition: To move skillfully or carefully.

Sentence: Bats have developed techniques to maneuver effectively in the dark.

3. prey /preɪ/

Definition: An animal that is hunted and killed by another for food.

Sentence: Bats rely on echolocation to detect their prey, such as moths and beetles.

4. sonar /ˈsəʊnɑːr/

Definition: A system for detecting objects under water or in the air using sound waves.

Sentence: Bats use a natural form of sonar to navigate and find food in the dark.

5. echolocation /ˌɛkəʊləʊˈkeɪʃən/

Definition: The use of sound waves and echoes to determine the location of objects.

Sentence: Echolocation allows bats to map their environment and avoid obstacles in total darkness.

6. obstacle /ˈɒbstəkəl/

Definition: Something that blocks or hinders movement or progress.

Sentence: Bats must detect and avoid obstacles like trees and walls during flight.

7. detectable /dɪˈtɛktəbl/

Definition: Able to be discovered or noticed.

Sentence: The sound waves emitted by bats bounce back, making objects detectable in the dark.

8. peripheral /pəˈrɪfərəl/

Definition: Relating to or situated on the edge of something.

Sentence: Bats rely on both direct and peripheral hearing to identify their surroundings.

9. illuminate /ɪˈluːmɪneɪt/

Definition: To light up or make something visible.

Sentence: Unlike bats, humans rely on artificial light to illuminate their surroundings at night.

10. scatter /ˈskætər/

Definition: To disperse or spread widely in different directions.

Sentence: Light in muddy water is often scattered, making it hard to see.

11. uncanny /ʌnˈkæni/

Definition: Strange or mysterious, especially in an unsettling way.

Sentence: Blind humans sometimes seem to have an uncanny ability to sense obstacles through echoes.

12. emit /ɪˈmɪt/

Definition: To produce or discharge something, especially gas or sound.

Sentence: Bats emit high-pitched sounds to locate objects in their environment.

13. instinct /ˈɪnstɪŋkt/

Definition: An innate, typically fixed pattern of behavior in animals in response to certain stimuli.

Sentence: Bats’ ability to navigate in the dark relies on both learned behavior and natural instinct.
